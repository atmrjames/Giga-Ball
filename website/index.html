Giga-Ball

Smash bricks. Catch power-ups. Climb forever.

Giga-Ball is a fast, colourful brick-breaker for iPhone and iPad. Battle through handcrafted level packs in Classic Mode, chase your best height in two endless modes, and take on a fresh Daily Challenge with new twists every day. Dozens of power-ups, Game Center leaderboards and achievements, and progress that syncs across all your devices with iCloud.
